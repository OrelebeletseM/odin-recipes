Pizza

Description

This homemade thin-crust pizza features a crispy yet chewy crust topped with rich tomato sauce, melted cheese, and your favorite toppings. Made from scratch with simple ingredients, it's perfect for pizza night with family and friends.

The dough is easy to work with and bakes up beautifully in a hot oven, giving you that authentic pizzeria experience at home. Customize it with your favorite toppings for a meal everyone will love.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup lukewarm water
  • 1 teaspoon active dry yeast
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour, plus more for kneading
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 to 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Optional toppings: pepperoni, mushrooms, bell peppers, onions, cooked sausage

Steps

  1. Arrange a rack in the bottom third of the oven, place a rimmed baking sheet on the rack, and heat the oven to 450°F.
  2. Pour lukewarm water into a bowl and sprinkle yeast over it. Let stand until foamy, 3 to 5 minutes.
  3. Add flour and salt. Mix until a shaggy dough forms, then knead on a work surface until smooth and tacky, 5 to 8 minutes.
  4. Cover and let rest for 10 minutes while preparing toppings.
  5. Divide dough into 2 pieces and stretch or roll each into a 10- to 12-inch round.
  6. Carefully remove the preheated baking sheet from the oven and place upside down. Transfer dough onto it.
  7. Brush dough with olive oil, spread with sauce, and sprinkle with cheese and toppings.
  8. Bake until crust is golden and cheese is bubbly, 8 to 12 minutes. Cool 5 minutes before serving.
