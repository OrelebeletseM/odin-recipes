Chocolate Cake

Description

This decadent chocolate cake is incredibly moist, rich, and fudgy with a deep chocolate flavor. Topped with a silky chocolate fudge frosting, it's the ultimate dessert for chocolate lovers and special occasions.

Made with simple ingredients and no mixer required for the cake, this recipe is foolproof and delivers impressive results every time. One reader called it the "Best chocolate cake ever!" [citation:2].

Ingredients

  • 3 oz unsweetened chocolate, melted
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
  • 2 1/4 cups light brown sugar, packed
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 1/4 cups sifted cake flour
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup boiling water

Chocolate Fudge Frosting:

  • 5 oz semi-sweet chocolate
  • 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 Tbsp milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 7 1/2 cups confectioners' sugar

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Butter and flour three 8-inch round cake pans.
  2. Melt unsweetened chocolate in microwave in 30-second intervals; set aside to cool.
  3. In a large bowl, cream butter until smooth. Add brown sugar and eggs; beat until light and fluffy.
  4. Beat in vanilla, melted chocolate, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Add flour alternately with sour cream, beating until smooth.
  6. Stir in boiling water until combined (batter will be thin).
  7. Pour into prepared pans and bake for 20-25 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
  8. Cool in pans for 10-15 minutes, then transfer to wire rack to cool completely.

For the Frosting:

  1. Place chocolate and cream in a microwave-safe bowl. Microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring after each, until smooth (about 1 minute). Cool for 30 minutes.
  2. Beat butter and chocolate mixture until smooth. Add vanilla and milk.
  3. Gradually add confectioners' sugar, beating until smooth. Add more milk if needed for spreading consistency.
  4. Frost the cooled cake immediately.
